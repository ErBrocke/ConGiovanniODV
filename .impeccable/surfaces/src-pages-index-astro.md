# Surface: Home (/) and edition pages (/premio/[anno]/)

Mode: Persuade (come to the Premio, give, help). Audience: Livornese friends/family on phones from WhatsApp/Instagram; Conservatorio students and parents; local press. Action: know the next Premio (date, place, free), add it to the calendar, give by bank transfer. Proof: the real 2025 running order (Sophia Rizzo: Scriabin op. 19, Chopin op. 25 n. 1–2; Claudio Monteleone: Tagliagole), named winners of every edition, press links, family photos of Giovanni. Constraints: no CMS, Italian only, no invented facts; photos supplied by the family.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the printed programma di sala of the Premio evening, a one-ink job in Giovanni's ultramarine on uncoated white card, in the manner of Italian modernist theatre programmes. It refuses the memorial-site default (soft portrait, cream, serif, donate button) and the event-landing default (hero, cards, countdown).

OWN-WORLD: Two materials only: ultramarine ink (the logo ring, #020e7e) and cool white uncoated card. The cover and back cover are drenched in ink; interior pages are paper printed in ink. Every photo is a one-ink duotone. Archivo (variable width) set condensed and heavy for titles, normal width for text; Courier Prime for typed notes, captions and credits, echoing the logo's typewriter ring. Hairline rules, dotted leaders, a typed running head and folio on every printed page; a full-bleed duotone plate between the dedication and the ink insert.

STORY: The visitor holds this year's programme: sees the IV Premio cover with date, place and "ingresso libero"; reads what the evening is through last year's real running order; meets Giovanni on the dedication pages; scans every edition's winners; turns to the back pages to give.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed ink cover. Top: typed "Con Giovanni ODV presenta" left, contents links right. Left 60%: "Premio Giovanni Capanna" stacked in condensed heavy paper-white at up to 9.5rem (62% width, uppercase; the programme-cover world overrides the 6rem display max here and for the 17rem edition numeral on edition pages; interior headings stay at or under 6rem). Right: the logo roundel, about a third of the width. Bottom: a hairline-ruled band with date, Auditorium del Conservatorio Mascagni and a typed line "IV edizione · ingresso libero · …", and the perforated paper ticket "Segna la data" (downloads .ics) as the primary action, "Sostieni il Premio" as text link beside it.

FORM: Programma di sala, candidate 1 of 7 (the user's pick over the assigned scout-badge direction); seed key 0e43bf9f. Signature interaction: the ticket stub tears along its perforation and stays torn (stub shifts, perforation opens) while the calendar file downloads; reduced motion jumps to the torn state.
