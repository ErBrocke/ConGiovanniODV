## Platform

## Users

- **Livorno friends and family of Giovanni Capanna**, mostly adults 40–75, arriving on a phone from a WhatsApp forward or the Instagram account @con_giovanni. They want to know when the next Premio is, where, whether it's free, and how to give.
- **Young musicians and actors, their teachers and parents**, especially at the Conservatorio Pietro Mascagni. They look for past winners and what the Premio means.
- **Local press and partners** (Il Tirreno, QuiLivorno, Livornopress, the Conservatorio) who need the facts of each edition.

## Product Purpose

Website of **Con Giovanni ODV**, a volunteer association (organizzazione di volontariato) founded on 4 November 2024 by Giovanni Capanna's family and friends. It keeps his memory alive by doing what he cared about: supporting young Livornese artists in music and theatre, promoting study and cultural activity, funding research on metastatic pancreatic cancer, and running solidarity initiatives.

Success: people come to the Premio evening, give by bank transfer, offer to help, and young artists see the Premio as worth applying for and being named in.

## Positioning

The **Premio Giovanni Capanna** is the association's signature: since 2023, every year, a cash award to young Livornese musicians and theatre artists, given at a free evening in the Auditorium of the Conservatorio Mascagni, where the winners perform. Memory turned into a stage for the next generation. It is personal and local: the family and friends of one funny, self-ironic Livornese who played music and acted.

## Operating Context

- Editions so far: 3 Dec 2023, 1 Dec 2024 (18:00), 29 Nov 2025 (17:00), all at the Auditorium del Conservatorio Mascagni, Livorno. Fourth edition: 28 Nov 2026, same venue, time and winners to be announced.
- The association also runs fundraising dinners (e.g. Cena Degustazione, June 2026, Birrificio Clandestino) — not on the site this round.
- Announcements travel through Instagram, WhatsApp and local press.

## Product Principles

1. The next Premio and how to come or give are always one glance away.
2. Giovanni is present as a person, with his humour, not as an obituary.
3. The young artists are the protagonists of each edition, named and credited.
4. Every fact is checkable; nothing is invented about real people.
